About

A perspective shaped by a decade in healthcare.

A professional consulting approach that knows the sector from the inside and values relationships between organisations.

KK
Kerim KahramanBursa

Profile

With 10 years of experience in the healthcare sector, Kerim Kahraman works in business development, corporate communication and consulting, drawing on his professional background in private healthcare organisations and his relationships across the sector.

Experience gained within private healthcare organisations makes it possible to view an institution as a whole — from day-to-day operations and processes spanning patient admission to corporate communication, to the relationships between stakeholders across the sector.

Today, this experience is offered as consulting support to healthcare organisations and companies in the sector, helping them turn growth objectives into realistic steps and strengthen their corporate relationships.

Approach

Listen, understand, plan together.

Every organisation has its own structure, culture and priorities. That is why the consulting process is shaped by the organisation’s own conditions and goals — not by ready-made templates.

The aim is to deliver clear, actionable recommendations that the organisation’s own teams can sustain, rather than complex reports.

About the scope of servicesOur consulting addresses the business and management processes of organisations. Medical diagnosis, treatment, patient referral and medical tourism brokerage are outside the scope of these services.
